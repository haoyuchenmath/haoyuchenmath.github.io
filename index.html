I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Utah. Previously, I obtained my M.S. in Statistics from the University of Minnesota in 2021 and my B.A. in Statistics from UC Berkeley in 2019. I am a U.S. citizen.

My research focuses on geometry and manifold learning. I develop interpretable geometric models and distance metrics grounded in rigorous theory and supported by efficient algorithms. I use geometric perspectives to gain insight, improve model performance, and design methods that are mathematically principled and computationally practical.
